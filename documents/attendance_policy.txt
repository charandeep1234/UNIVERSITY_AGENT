SMART UNIVERSITY ATTENDANCE POLICY AND REGULATIONS

1. MINIMUM ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent in each registered theory and laboratory subject during the semester to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.

2. DETENTION AND INELIGIBILITY
Students whose attendance falls below the mandatory 75 percent requirement in any course will not be permitted to appear for the semester end examinations in that specific subject and will be marked as detained. Detained students must re-register for the course in subsequent semesters.

3. MEDICAL CONDONATION RULES
In cases of genuine medical illness or hospitalization, an attendance relaxation of up to 10 percent (condonation minimum 65 percent) may be granted by the Dean of Academic Affairs.
- Medical certificates and leave applications must be submitted to the Head of Department within 3 working days of returning to campus.
- A standard medical condonation processing fee must be deposited upon approval.

4. DUTY LEAVE AND CO-CURRICULAR PARTICIPATION
Students representing the university in national/international competitions, sports tournaments, technical fests, or official university delegations are eligible for on-duty (OD) attendance credit upon prior approval from the faculty advisor and Dean.

5. ATTENDANCE MONITORING
Attendance is recorded digitally by faculty members in every lecture and laboratory session and updated weekly on the University Student ERP Portal. Students and parents are advised to check attendance records regularly.
