SMART UNIVERSITY FEES INFORMATION AND PAYMENT GUIDELINES

1. TUITION FEE STRUCTURE AND PAYMENT MODES
- Tuition fees, laboratory fees, and library charges are payable per semester.
- Payment Modes: Students must make all payments online via the Official University Finance Portal (https://fees.smartuniv.edu).
- Accepted payment methods: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking, Debit Cards, and Credit Cards with zero transaction surcharge.
- Cash payments at the university counter are strictly prohibited for safety and auditing compliance.

2. PAYMENT DEADLINES
- Autumn (Odd) Semester: Due on or before August 15.
- Spring (Even) Semester: Due on or before January 15.
- An official electronic payment receipt with a digital QR verification code is generated immediately upon successful transaction.

3. LATE FEE SLABS AND PENALTIES
- Days 1 to 7 past deadline: Late fine of $50 (Rs. 1,000).
- Days 8 to 15 past deadline: Late fine of $100 (Rs. 2,500).
- Beyond 15 days delay: Temporary suspension of ERP portal access, library privileges, and examination hall ticket issuance until full clearance.

4. INSTALLMENT OPTIONS AND SCHOLARSHIPS
- Installment Payment: Students facing financial constraints may request fee payment in two equal installments by submitting a formal request to the Finance Office 14 days before the primary deadline.
- Merit and Need-Based Scholarships: Scholarship deductions ranging from 25% to 100% of tuition fee are automatically adjusted in the semester fee demand note for eligible students maintaining a minimum CGPA of 8.5.

5. REFUND POLICY
Withdrawal before the commencement of classes is eligible for a 90% tuition refund. No refund is granted for course withdrawals initiated 30 days after semester commencement.
