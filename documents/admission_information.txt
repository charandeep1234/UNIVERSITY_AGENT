SMART UNIVERSITY ADMISSION CRITERIA AND DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

1. UNDERGRADUATE ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- Minimum qualification: Successful completion of 10+2 / Higher Secondary Certificate / Equivalent Examination with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science.
- Academic cutoff: Minimum aggregate of 60% marks in PCM/CS for general category candidates (55% for reserved categories).
- Entrance Examination: Valid merit rank in National/State Entrance Tests (e.g., JEE Main / State CET) or Smart University Entrance Examination (SUEE).

2. MANDATORY DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR ADMISSION
Candidates must produce original documents along with 3 sets of self-attested photocopies during counseling:
1. Class 10th Certificate & Marksheet (as proof of Date of Birth).
2. Class 12th / Equivalent Qualifying Examination Marksheet and Pass Certificate.
3. Entrance Examination Admit Card and Official Rank Card / Scorecard.
4. Transfer Certificate (TC) and Conduct/Character Certificate from the last attended institution.
5. Migration Certificate (for students coming from boards/universities other than the state board).
6. Government Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License).
7. Category/Caste Certificate and Income Certificate (if applying under affirmative quota/scholarship schemes).
8. 6 Recent passport-sized color photographs with white background.
9. Medical Fitness Certificate signed by a registered medical practitioner.

3. ADMISSION PROCESS AND COUNSELING
- Step 1: Submit online application at https://admissions.smartuniv.edu.
- Step 2: Verification of uploaded documents by the Admissions Cell.
- Step 3: Merit list publication and seat allocation based on preferences.
- Step 4: Payment of admission confirmation fee and physical document verification on campus.
