SMART UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC CALENDAR (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)

1. ODD SEMESTER (AUTUMN TERM: AUGUST - DECEMBER 2026)
- August 1, 2026: Commencement of Odd Semester classes for all undergraduate and postgraduate programs.
- August 15, 2026: Independence Day (Holiday) & Last date for Semester Fee Payment without fine.
- September 22 to September 28, 2026: Mid-Term Continuous Assessment Tests (CAT-1 / Internal Exams).
- October 2, 2026: Gandhi Jayanti (University Holiday).
- October 19 to October 24, 2026: Dussehra & Diwali Festival Break.
- November 3 to November 8, 2026: Pre-Final Internal Assessments & Practical Laboratory Viva.
- November 10, 2026: Last instruction day of Odd Semester classes.
- November 18 to December 12, 2026: Semester End Theory Examinations.
- December 15, 2026 to January 3, 2027: Winter Vacation & Faculty Evaluation Break.

2. EVEN SEMESTER (SPRING TERM: JANUARY - MAY 2027)
- January 4, 2027: Commencement of Even Semester classes.
- January 15, 2027: Last date for Even Semester Fee Payment without fine.
- January 26, 2027: Republic Day Celebration.
- February 20 to February 22, 2027: Annual University Cultural Fest ('AURA 2027') & Inter-Collegiate Sports Meet.
- March 15 to March 20, 2027: Mid-Term Internal Examinations (CAT-2).
- April 15, 2027: Last day of instruction for Even Semester.
- April 25 to May 20, 2027: Even Semester End Comprehensive Examinations.
- May 25 to June 30, 2027: Summer Internship Period & Remedial/Supplementary Exams.
- July 10, 2027: Annual Convocation Ceremony and Degree Conferral.
