SMART UNIVERSITY EXAMINATION RULES AND GUIDELINES

1. CONDUCT OF SEMESTER EXAMINATIONS
Semester end examinations are conducted twice per academic year:
- Odd Semester Examinations: Conducted during November and December.
- Even Semester Examinations: Conducted during April and May.
Supplementary and improvement examinations are scheduled during the summer break (June/July).

2. HALL TICKET AND IDENTIFICATION
- Every student must carry a valid University Identity Card and official Hall Ticket (Admit Card) to the examination center.
- Hall tickets can be downloaded from the university portal 1 week prior to the examination start date, provided fee dues and minimum attendance requirements are cleared.
- Entry without a verified Hall Ticket and ID card is strictly prohibited.

3. EXAMINATION TIMINGS AND REPORTING
- Morning session runs from 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM; afternoon session runs from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
- Candidates must report to the assigned examination hall at least 20 minutes before the scheduled start time.
- Late entry beyond 15 minutes after commencement is not permitted under any circumstances.
- No candidate may leave the examination hall during the first 60 minutes of the examination.

4. CODE OF CONDUCT AND PROHIBITED ITEMS
- Strictly Prohibited: Mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, electronic memory devices, books, and unauthorized written materials.
- All bags and personal electronic gadgets must be deposited at designated cloakrooms before entering.

5. MALPRACTICE AND DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
Any attempt to copy, exchange materials, impersonate, or communicate with other candidates constitutes severe malpractice. The University Disciplinary Action Committee reserves the right to cancel examination papers, suspend the candidate, or debar the student for up to two academic terms.

6. RESULTS AND REVALUATION
Results are declared within 30 days of the last examination. Students seeking photocopy or revaluation of answer scripts must apply through the portal within 10 days of results publication along with the prescribed revaluation fee.
